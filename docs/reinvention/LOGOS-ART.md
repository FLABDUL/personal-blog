## Provenance and boundaries

The conceptual references are [geometric patterns](https://www.metmuseum.org/fr/essays/geometric-patterns-in-islamic-art) and [vegetal patterns in Islamic art](https://www.metmuseum.org/es/essays/vegetal-patterns-in-islamic-art), documented by the Metropolitan Museum of Art. These are broad design references, not claims that every concept reproduces a historical style. No source image or inscription from the user's screenshot was copied, downloaded or traced. No private or draft artwork was used.

Only E contains Arabic: Hakim's confirmed personal name حكيم, without the definite article or any sacred text. It was visually checked for the intended four letters and two ya dots. Professional calligraphy proof remains pending. The other four use Latin lettering.

The sheets were visually inspected for distinctness, name spelling and layout. They differ in geometry, silhouette, composition, lettering, colour and ornament density. B and E retain illustrative texture from generation; their production versions need deliberate vector simplification. These are raster concept sheets, not finished logos. Small studies are design suggestions, not proof that every fine detail survives at favicon size. No trademark clearance was performed.
