## Cultural reference and lettering boundaries

- The requested Arabic is exactly the four-letter personal name ح ك ي م, joined as حكيم. No definite article, divine title, sacred inscription or copied calligraphy is included.
- Green is Hakim's requested personal association with Islam; the page does not claim a universal religious meaning for a colour.
- C takes an original geometric direction from Malaysian songket craft. [UNESCO's songket entry](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/songket-01505) documents the Malaysian tradition and its geometric and nature-inspired patterns. The concept is not presented as an authentic historical motif or a reproduction of a particular textile.
- The attached search-results screenshot was context only. No stock artwork, watermarked image or external calligraphy was copied or downloaded.
- All three presentation sheets were created with the built-in image-generation tool. No CLI/API fallback was used. The AI provenance is visible on the review page.
- The lettering was visually checked for the four intended letters, two ya dots and absence of the prefix. B initially had a broken join before its final mim; a targeted image edit corrected that join in all three copies. This is not a professional calligrapher's proof.
- Final calligraphy proof, vector redraw, small-size dot spacing, masthead use in both themes, and a dedicated favicon remain after a choice. These raster presentation sheets are concepts, not production logo assets. No trademark clearance was performed.

Original PNGs are preserved in ignored `output/reinvention/logos/jade-signature.png`, `structured-script.png` and `woven-roots.png`, as well as the image tool's original output directory. Sharp only resized and encoded the generated sheets; creative edits used the image tool.

### A: Jade Signature

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE original personal portfolio logo presentation for Made by Hakim. Direction A: JADE SIGNATURE. Exact Arabic word, the person's given name only: حكيم . Spell it with exactly these FOUR base letters in normal Arabic right-to-left order: ح then ك then ي then م. Natural connected Arabic shaping. Two distinct dots below the medial ya. No definite article or extra initial letters. This is a person's name, not a divine name or religious inscription. Set the word large and beautifully legible in a refined flowing contemporary Naskh-inspired calligraphic hand with gentle pen contrast, restrained elegant curves, not an ornate tangle. The Arabic word is the entire hero mark. Beneath it, much smaller understated serif text exactly 'made by hakim'. Deep jade green #175444 on flat warm ivory #f6f1e5. A single quiet horizontal composition centred on a wide landscape sheet with generous whitespace. In the lower third, repeat the SAME exact Arabic word alone at two smaller scales as size studies. No monogram, m/h, Latin H icon, crescent, star, mosque, dome, halo, seal, scripture, quotations, translations, title, decorative letterlike strokes, extra Arabic words, diacritics, mockup objects, gradient or texture. The word must be clearly حكيم, with no prefix. Confident personal artist/engineer signature. Output landscape.
