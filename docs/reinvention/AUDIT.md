## Live audit

All seven sections inspected in the in-app browser. The common black background, blue accents, rounded containers and Arial typography overwhelm differences in content. About has a small circular portrait and no strong entry point. Blog and Projects flatten hierarchy into equal cards. Experience is useful but visually dense. Studio reduces nine expressive paintings to thumbnails with no close viewing. Bookshelf's cover imagery is appealing; Music's authored atlas is worth preserving. Live Music displayed a catalogue-unavailable fallback during the audit.

## Three directions

### A. Collected Works — selected

A portfolio as an evolving body of work. Warm paper, decisive typography, index numbers, generous space and a consistent masthead. Each discipline earns a setting: editorial introduction, blue engineering workbench, readable journal, career ledger, face-out library, listening atlas and art exhibition. Content supplies imagery. Spatial hierarchy replaces decorative cards.

Best balance of identity, clarity, performance and growth. Expressive without fictional room imagery or invented biography.

### B. House of Making

A room-like entrance, object navigation, writing table, workbench and salon wall. Tactile and memorable, but risks puzzling navigation and invented imagery. A full About alternative tests a flatter, accessible specimen-table interpretation.

### C. Signal & Matter

High-contrast typography, dark surfaces, electric accents and large image fields. Strong affinity with software/music, but an entire site in this mode would compromise long-form reading. A full Studio alternative tests a daylight contact-sheet exhibition against the selected dark gallery.

## Implementation criteria

- Preserve existing routes, factual content and useful interactions.
- No external artwork or new claims about personal experience.
- Everything accessible without hover; focus, touch and reduced motion work.
- Review alternatives are real routes, clearly labelled and removable with one configuration change.
- Prioritise the integrated candidate.
- Never push, merge, promote or redeploy production.
